- The Go reconciler publishes both top-level and condition observedGeneration from the evaluated Greeting generation. It requires the child Deployment's current observedGeneration and desired updated/ready/available counts, returns read/write/status errors and avoids writing unchanged status. Existing fake tests cover these branches; they do not implement an API-server or kubelet.
- `SetControllerReference` in controller-runtime 0.23.1 sets controller ownership by UID and blockOwnerDeletion. The checked-in Role separately grants update on `greetings/finalizers`; the official 1.35 admission-controller contract confirms why this permission is needed. No broad delete grant was added to the running controller.
- Children and primary share a namespace. Namespace deletion and owner-reference cascading are distinct Kubernetes mechanisms; neither requires an external-resource finalizer in this stateless example. A stalled real deletion still requires observing finalizers and permissions, not claiming the fake client proved garbage collection.
- Admission registration is deleted before its backend namespace. Conversion resources and CRD are deleted while conversion is still available. Shared Greeting CRD removal remains conditional on a cluster-wide instance inventory. CSI/etcd ownership guards refuse cleanup after failed preflight without a matching run marker. No cleanup command was executed by the reviewer.
